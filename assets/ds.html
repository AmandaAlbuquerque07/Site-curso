Context rules

Ink carries text, borders and pressed states. Paper is the default canvas. Acid is deliberately scarce: focus, success and decisive calls-to-action. Air and Blush only participate at 10–60% opacity inside gradients, never as body text. Disabled uses Ink at 30% over Paper.
